# Direction

Monkeyspeak trains people to compose and speak effective prompts quickly, with less unnecessary repetition. It takes inspiration from Monkeytype's short practice sessions and personal bests. Monkeyspeak is a working name; collaborators are workshopping a replacement.

The round starts by presenting an idea for an app. The player thinks, speaks a prompt, and receives grades for time, duplication, and usefulness. Usefulness includes how well the player thought through the idea; a generic request for the agent to figure everything out should score poorly. [The scoring ticket](https://github.com/mia-cx/monkeyspeak/issues/2) owns the candidate rubric. Its weights, clock rules, and qualification for personal bests remain open decisions.

Prefer local browser inference. Evaluate tr33town/ok.yay's existing WebGPU framework first; its repository and access details still need recording. Bit-chat and WebLLM are references. The feasibility of speech recognition, phone loading, and consistent small-model grading remains to be established. Fine-tuning is a possibility, not a requirement.

The current milestone is a buildable first-version agreement. [The wayfinder map](https://github.com/mia-cx/monkeyspeak/issues/1) owns the decision tickets and their resolutions. Contributors can claim unblocked tickets now. Product implementation follows those decisions.

The repository has no application implementation yet. Keep experiments separate and link their evidence from the relevant ticket.
